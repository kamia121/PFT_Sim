PFT Sim QuizCase practice page

Quizzes and assessments

Randomized simulated pulmonary function reports with structured questions. Practice gives feedback after each question; an exam code gives every learner the same cases with feedback held until submission; the instructor tab builds the code and the printed sheets.

Scope: the quiz asks the FEV₁ and FVC quality grades as items of their own. The study-level and maneuver-level quality reviews are on the case practice page and do not apply here.

Educational use only. Cases are simulated and uncalibrated. This tool does not provide patient-treatment recommendations and must not be used for clinical decisions.
