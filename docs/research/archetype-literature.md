# Generator parameters against the literature (research thread report, 2026-10-20)

Source of this file: the final report of a research thread that read the code (`src/core/physiology.js`, `descriptor.js`) and searched the literature. Saved here unchanged in substance so that the decisions in `docs/archetype-changes.md` have an audit trail. The decisions are not in this file; this file is the evidence and the thread's recommendations, and the thread's recommendations were reviewed critically (see the decision document).

Method and limits stated by the thread. "Code" values were measured by running `generatePhysiology` for 600 seeds per archetype and severity (White, GLI anchor, pre-bronchodilator) and converting to percent predicted (%p) with the repository's own `resolveReference`. Cohort %p were converted to GLI z for typical patients (M 63 y/175 cm, F 63 y/162 cm, and others); for example DLCO 80% is z -1.2 to -1.5, 63% is -2.4 to -3.0, 51% is -3.4 to -4.2, 40% is -4.4 to -5.6. Cohorts used other equations (ECCS, NHANES III, van der Lee, mostly unnamed in the text), so those conversions carry an uncertainty of about 0.5 z, and %p from a cohort is not comparable with %p under GLI Global without that conversion. Evidence marks: T = table read in full text, A = abstract only. Not read: ASCEND, INPULSIS, SENSCIS and INBUILD baseline tables, the ATS/ERS 2017 DLCO standard, Jones and Nzekwu tables, COPDGene or SPIROMICS baseline by GOLD stage. Not found: correlations among z-scores (factor loadings), VA/TLC by GOLD stage, any denominator-based practice mix. Not checked: the hemoglobin, height and BMI draws.

Practice mix. The weights (normal 14, obstruction 22, parenchymal 10, extraparenchymal 6, mixed 8, nonspecific 8, low-DLCO 7, anemia 4, obesity 7, BD-discordant 8) are not prevalence; no denominator-based mix was found, so the "teaching choice" label stays. The only denominator figure is Hyatt's nonspecific share of 9.5% of tests at Mayo (A). General-population PRISm is 8.5% of 53,701 (Wan 2021, A, with obesity 48.3% vs 31.4%) and 12.4% of COPDGene smokers (Wan 2018, A). Because PRISm tracks obesity, nonspecific cases could draw higher BMI.

Constrained rather than empirical. Identities (not parameters): TLC = RV + VC; FRC = RV + ERV and IC = VC - ERV; FEV1 at most FVC (ratio cap 0.98, post-BD cap 0.985); DLCO = KCO x VA and VA at most TLC; the raised RV in the nonspecific case follows from normal TLC and low VC; the Cotes form of the Hb correction; the KCO and VA relation (Johnson); z = -1.645 and the severity cut points are conventions. Modeling clamps: ERV floor 0.12 L and cap 0.85 VC; VA/TLC clipped to 0.8 to 0.96; FVC/VC gap at least 0 (not physical); the `valueAt` clamp. Without a source: all truncation SDs; the emphysema VA shift (VA/TLC -9% to -17%; qualitative support in Hughes); most ERV and RV z means.
