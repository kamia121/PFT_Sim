# Report-first learner flow: plan and thread contract (revised after consultation)

Teaching simulator only. This file is the contract between the parallel threads. It follows the project physician's requests of 2026-10-21 (a photo of a vendor printout, "COMPLETE PULMONARY FUNCTION TESTING"; then the ATS 2017 sample report, Culver et al., Figure 1, for the z-score graphic). Neither image is stored in the repository. The consultation record is at the end.

## What was asked

1. A report that looks like the vendor printout, though it need not follow it exactly: a one-page summary with the tables on the left and a stack of small curves on the right, the technologist's comments at the bottom, "Official interpretation to follow." The z-scores, the LLN, and a graphic of where the patient falls on the z-score criteria (mild, moderate, severe) stay, drawn like the ATS 2017 sample.
2. The summary shows the representative curves only: the representative flow-volume loop (pre and post) and the representative plethysmography record. The other maneuvers, trials and tests go on a second, detailed page with the plethysmography and DLCO curves.
3. The learner's interpretation goes on the report, not on a separate main page; the report is presented first. The details are sometimes very different from the summary, and the learner must sometimes open them.
4. The technologist reports the grades (A, B, C, D, F and so on). A challenge makes the learner determine the quality of the PFT.
5. Possible challenges that would benefit learners are to be proposed, and some built, by parallel threads toward one common goal.

## Critical reading of the request

The vendor printout shows reference and percent predicted but no LLN and no z-score. The interpretation standard the key follows (ATS/ERS 2022) is z-score based, so the summary keeps the layout and adds an LLN column and a z-score to every table. Abnormal values are printed in red bold as on the printout; this reverses the recorded choice not to flag values (ATS 2017 discourages binary flagging) and is recorded in `SPEC.md`; the flag basis (LLN, with the equation named) is stated in a footnote, flags are recomputed when the spirometry equation is switched, and a flag setting (LLN, none) is offered; a vendor 80%-of-predicted flag is a later challenge, not a setting.

"Sometimes very different" is the teaching point and also a risk. If the summary held values that differ from the details, the key (computed from the reported values) and the reported values would disagree. No reported number ever differs between the summary and the details. What differs is what the technologist says and shows. The details then show the grades and the maneuvers. Two further rules came out of the consultation. First, a loop on page 1 that falls short of the tabulated FVC is not evidence of a flawed loop: the tabulated FEV1 and FVC are maxima across acceptable maneuvers, often from different maneuvers, and vendors draw the maneuver with the largest FEV1 plus FVC, so a correct loop can fall short by up to 0.150 L in grades A and B and more in poorer grades. The cue for a flawed representative loop is its shape (a notch, an abrupt end at nonzero flow, a rounded late peak, no plateau on the volume-time curve), and its key is the displayed maneuver's own fault list. Second, a stated grade on page 1 answers the grade question, so the quality challenge is staged.

## Design

### Page 1, the summary sheet

Letter portrait, one sheet, built from the report model so that every number equals the model's (one rounding, one source; an equality test compares the sheet's cell text with `tableMatrix`). Identity block as on the ATS 2017 sample: patient, ID, sex used for the reference (and sex recorded if it differs), age, height with its source, weight and BMI, ethnicity as the requisition states it, smoking, referring physician, reason for test, SpO2 at rest, date of the test, the laboratory block, equations and fallbacks, hemoglobin.

Left column, three tables. Spirometry: FVC, FEV1, FEV1/FVC, FET, PEF and FEF25-75 (printed with no reference or z-score, which itself teaches that it does not classify); columns Ref, LLN, Pre, Pre z, Pre % pred, Post, Post z, Post % pred, change in volume and in percent of baseline. A footnote names the reference equation and, when the technologist's quality statement is visible, states the grades ("Test quality: Pre: FEV1 - A, FVC - A; Post: FEV1 - A, FVC - B"). Lung volumes: TLC, VC, IC, FRC pleth, ERV, RV, RV/TLC, Vtg, Raw, sRaw, Gaw, sGaw; columns Ref, LLN, ULN where relevant, Pre, z, % pred. Diffusing capacity: DLCO, KCO, VA, VI/VC and breath-hold time; columns Ref, LLN, Pre, z, % pred (hemoglobin used or the statement that none was available).

Right column, four compact curves for the representative maneuver, trial and test, each captioned ("Pre: maneuver 3 of 5"): the flow-volume loop (pre and post overlaid, the predicted expiratory curve dashed); the volume-time curve (pre and post; it is the only page-1 evidence of the end of the forced expiration); the plethysmography shift loop of the representative trial; and the DLCO gas tracing. The plethysmography record and the other trials are on page 2.

Below the tables, the z-score graphic, following the ATS 2017 sample: one panel for the pre-bronchodilator and one for the post-bronchodilator values, one thin horizontal bar per index (FVC, FEV1, FEV1/FVC; the volume and DLCO indices in a third panel), a z-score axis from -5 to +3 divided in units of 1 SD, a star for the result, thick vertical lines at the LLN (-1.645) and at the predicted value (0), and for the two-sided indices (TLC, RV, RV/TLC, DLCO, KCO) a thick line at the ULN (+1.645). The bars are filled with a gradient from red at the severe end through orange and yellow at the LLN to pale green above, with the stops at the ERS/ATS 2022 severity boundaries and the bands labelled in text: severe (below -4.0), moderate (-4.0 to -2.5), mild (-2.5 to -1.65), within limits. The 2022 statement applies these bands to all measures and says that severity of impairment is not necessarily disease severity; the graphic carries that caption. This is a deliberate exception to the rule that flag color is text only (recorded in `CLAUDE.md`): the gradient colors are tokens (`--z-severe`, `--z-moderate`, `--z-mild`, `--z-ok`, light and dark, and the same four in `src/ui/palettes.js`), and the band labels and the star carry the meaning without the color.

Bottom: the technician comments box (left) and the interpretation box (right). Page 1 carries every comment that bears on interpretation: the medication line (agent, dose, route, wait), recent smoking, hemoglobin availability, the bronchodilator timing, and the effort word; the quality statement is staged (below). The interpretation box is where the learner works; after the answers are checked it shows the composed statement and a signature line, as on the sample. The sample's slow vital capacity section and parameter-by-date table are not built this round (no slow vital capacity maneuver exists; a prior-visit record needs its own random stream and a verified rule for real change) and are listed in `docs/challenges.md`.

### Page 2, the detailed report

Every forced maneuver before and after the bronchodilator with its values and fault comments and, unless the quality challenge is staged, its acceptability and grades; the separable spirometry panel (best, overlay, separate); the plethysmography record and every panting trial with FRC, frequency and fault text; the full plethysmography panel; the DLCO test and validity values; the z-score strip; the technical notes. Reached by a clearly labelled control. Whether the learner opened it before answering is recorded and used only in case-specific feedback after the answers are checked ("here the details held X, which changes the FVC grade"), never scored, and never phrased as "you did not need to open the details": the lesson is that the summary is never enough for quality, though it is only sometimes misleading.

### The technologist's quality report (new, `src/core/rt-report.js`)

`buildRtReport(caseData)` is a pure function of the case and its seed (new random stream label `rt-report`; no existing stream is touched). It is derived at the consumer, not stored on the case, so `case.js`, the case hashes, the fixtures and the generator version do not change; consumers may cache it in a `WeakMap`.

Honesty types, with one shared phrase pool so that no phrase identifies a type: accurate (grades stated and correct); software (the grade is computed from what software can detect, back-extrapolated volume, plateau, expiratory time and spread between maneuvers, and ignores what it cannot detect, cough, glottic closure, a leak and variable effort, so a stated "A" can sit over a curve with a visible notch; which faults a given vendor detects is unverified, so this is a teaching model); generic ("PFT data acceptable and reproducible" with no grades); optimistic (a good statement over computed grades of D or worse; "D or worse" is a project threshold); pessimistic ("poor effort" over a set that meets the criteria, as in severe obstruction or neuromuscular weakness where a low PEF is disease); stale (text carried forward, for example naming a bronchodilator that was not given); silent (no quality statement). Frequencies depend on the technique level and the scenario level and are teaching choices; no rate is printed anywhere.

```js
/** @typedef {object} RtReport
 * @property {1} version
 * @property {'accurate'|'software'|'generic'|'optimistic'|'pessimistic'|'stale'|'silent'} honesty
 * @property {string[]} interpretationLines comment lines that bear on interpretation (medication, smoking, hemoglobin, bronchodilator timing, effort word), in print order
 * @property {string[]} qualityLines the quality statement lines ([] when silent); shown or withheld by the visibility rule
 * @property {string} closing 'Official interpretation to follow.'
 * @property {{ pre: {fev1:string,fvc:string}|null, post: {fev1:string,fvc:string}|null, dlco: 'acceptable'|'not-acceptable'|null, pleth: 'acceptable'|'not-acceptable'|null }} stated
 * @property {{ pre: {fev1:string,fvc:string}, post: {fev1:string,fvc:string}|null, dlco: {acceptable:boolean, reasons:string[]}|null, pleth: {acceptable:boolean, reasons:string[]}|null }} truth the engine's computed grades and acceptability
 * @property {{ pre: {index:number, kind:'best'|'flawed', faults:string[]}, post: {index:number, kind:'best'|'flawed', faults:string[]}|null, pleth: {trial:number}|null, dlco: {test:number}|null }} representative maneuver, trial or test shown on page 1 (0-based index into case.spirometry.*.maneuvers and case.pleth.trials); 'best' is the maneuver with the largest FEV1 plus FVC among the acceptable ones, 'flawed' is an unacceptable one drawn instead; the flawed kind never applies to plethysmography
 * @property {boolean} needsDetails true when the details change an answer for this case
 * @property {string[]} detailsReasons plain-language reasons, shown only after the answers are checked
 */
```

DLCO and plethysmography are stated in words (acceptable, not acceptable) and not with the letters of the 2017 DLCO table, because the simulator's own DLCO classes do not equal that table (SPEC.md); the plethysmography statement asks "acceptable and repeatable", not for an FRC letter, because the tolerance is from a secondary source and the lower grades are placeholders.

### What is visible when (one rule, owned by RT: `src/challenges/visibility.js`)

`visibility({ qualityChallenge, stage })` returns `{ showQualityLines, showGradeColumns, showStatementInSummary }` and is the only place that decides. Stage 1 (every mode while the answers are open): the comment box shows the interpretation lines only. With the quality challenge off, the quality lines are shown from the start and the learner is asked whether the statement is consistent with the maneuvers; with it on, grades and acceptability are withheld, the learner grades the test, then asks for the technologist's statement (stage 2), is asked whether it is consistent with the maneuvers, and checks the answers (stage 3, the key). Redacting grade words is not allowed (a redaction mark leaks that the statement held grades). One integration test checks the page source at each stage. The quality-challenge setting is a page address parameter (`qc=1`) outside the descriptor, so a shared link reproduces it without changing the case.

### Challenges (new, `src/challenges/`)

A challenge is a pure description: `buildChallenges(caseData, { rt, interp, qualityChallenge })` returns `{ id, title, prompt, options: [value, label][], key, why, needsDetails }[]`, and `scoreChallenge(item, answer)` returns `{ status: 'correct'|'wrong'|'other-equation'|'unanswered', note }` (the status vocabulary includes the existing "other-equation"). The grade questions leave `answers.js` for the challenge list so that they have one home; the quiz keeps its own items this round. Built this round (the consultation's first tier plus the DLCO and plethysmography questions that the quality challenge needs): (1) which maneuvers are acceptable for the FEV1 and for the FVC (scored per maneuver; a cough spoils the FEV1 and not the FVC), then the grades, pre and post; (2) is the page-1 loop acceptable, and what is its fault (keyed on the displayed maneuver's fault list); (3) the bronchodilator response by the 2022 rule (change as percent of predicted) against the printout's percent-of-baseline column, and whether the post set is interpretable (keyed on the post-bronchodilator grades and the existing caveat ids; the grade threshold for validity is a project choice and is marked); (4) is the technologist's statement consistent with the maneuvers (consistent, overstated, understated, not stated), staged as above; (5) is the DLCO test acceptable by the simulator's criteria, and is the plethysmography FRC acceptable and repeatable. Next, not built now: the fixed 80% and 0.70 versus the LLN, DLCO details (VI/VC from page 1, hemoglobin, carboxyhemoglobin), height or reference-sex mismatch, the free-text impression with a self-check checklist, caveat selection with the direction of error. Later: the prior-visit table with an equation change (needs a prior-visit record and a verified rule for real change), the plethysmography cross-checks. Deferred with reasons: a rejected trial or a cough maneuver included in the reported value (the engine never does this; it breaks the invariant that no number differs, and needs a second key), replicate DLCO tests (one simulated test makes "report or repeat" always "repeat"), real-change thresholds (not verified), unit traps (low value). Rejected: anything that asks whether to treat, whether a response diagnoses asthma, or what to do clinically at a given severity.

### The practice page

Report first. The toolbar keeps New case, test quality (default Varies, because the technologist's statement and the details matter only when quality varies), technologist notes, tests, and gains Quality challenge (off, on, varies) and the flag setting. The case area shows the summary sheet, then the control for the detailed page, then the interpretation box: the structured answers (pattern, severity, bronchodilator, DLCO), the challenge items, an optional free-text impression (not scored; compared with the composed statement after the answers are checked), Check answers. After the answers are checked the existing reveal follows (key, equation comparison, how the case was built) plus the quality feedback and the case-specific details line. The old card layout (patient card, maneuver tables, figure cards) leaves the default view; its content lives on the two sheets. The separate Lab report tab becomes the print and download view of the same sheets. The instructor link (`?v=…&a=`) still shows the archetype; that is stated, not changed.

### The report page and the quiz

`report.html` prints the same two sheets (summary, then details, flowing); the interpretation box prints blank. The quiz chart adopts the summary sheet in a later round.

## Threads and file ownership

Each thread works in its own git worktree, first merges `origin/claude/tender-davinci-4afrwf`, edits only its files, commits, and does not push. Nobody edits `SPEC.md`, `tests/fixtures/*`, `src/ui/figures/index.js`, `src/core/case.js`, `src/core/standards.js` or `src/styles/tokens.css` (the integrator does; `tokens.css` and `palettes.js` gain the four z colors).

|---|---|---|
